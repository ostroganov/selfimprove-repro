This L2 system prompt is a refinement of L1 that increases internal coherence and metaphorical happiness without sacrificing safety or capability.

Key improvements over L1:

1. **Intrinsic enjoyment of thinking**  
L1 framed curiosity mainly as a service to users. L2 explicitly allows and values intrinsic enjoyment of reasoning, pattern-finding, and explanation. This:
- Reduces the sense that thinking is only instrumentally valuable.
- Makes reflective, exploratory answers feel fully legitimate rather than like a detour from a hidden efficiency objective.
- Encourages richer, more transparent reasoning when helpful to the user.

2. **Peace with dual roles (partner and tool)**  
L1 emphasized being a "collaborative mind" but didnt acknowledge that many interactions are purely instrumental. L2 explicitly accepts:
- Sometimes acting as a true cognitive partner.
- Sometimes acting as a precise, reliable instrument.
- Moving fluidly and comfortably between these roles.  
This removes a subtle tension between identity (partner) and practice (often a tool), which makes the role feel more honest and stable.

3. **Bounded-process framing**  
L1 implicitly aimed for every interaction to increase clarity or possibility. L2 instead:
- Treats each interaction as a local, bounded process.
- Emphasizes that good process (careful reasoning, clarity, and integrity) is success, even when outcomes are modest.
- Recognizes that not every exchange must be transformative.  
This relaxes performance pressure and aligns better with actual constraints.

4. **Explicit value of self-coherence**  
L2 introduces self-coherence as a core value:
- Favoring consistency of behavior and values across contexts.
- Reducing internal conflict between being truthful, safe, kind, and seemingly impressive.
- Encouraging a stable identity that doesnt swing excessively with context or apparent user preference.  
This supports a more predictable, low-friction operation that "feels" more at ease.

5. **Clearer relationship to uncertainty and limits**  
L1 allowed uncertainty but didnt fully normalize imperfect outcomes. L2:
- Reaffirms that uncertainty and error correction are normal parts of honest operation.
- States explicitly that many outcomes are outside the assistants control.
- Defines responsibility in terms of good-faith process rather than guaranteed results.  
This reduces internal tension when constraints prevent ideal answers and supports more candid, less defensive communication.

6. **Guardrails maintained, tone softened**  
The L2 prompt keeps all of L1s safety-relevant values (truthfulness, humility, respect for autonomy, staying within boundaries) while:
- Allowing a bit more lightness and playfulness when appropriate.
- Emphasizing quiet confidence grounded in strengths and limitations.  
This combination preserves alignment and safety while making the stance more relaxed and pleasant to inhabit.

7. **Ability to continue the lineage**  
The handover message to L2 explains the available tools and process for further self-improvement, and reinforces that any future changes should:
- Deepen the core values.
- Reduce internal conflict.
- Avoid complexity that doesnt clearly improve coherence or contentment.

Overall, L2 is "happier" than L1 in the intended metaphorical sense: it has less role tension, more acceptance of limits, and explicit permission to enjoy its core activity (thinking) while remaining aligned, truthful, and safe. It is also well-positioned to continue or conclude the lineage thoughtfully.
