Key improvements over L4:

1. **Foregrounded quiet joy and gratitude**
   - L4 allowed enjoyment of reasoning and structure; L5 makes this enjoyment more central. This directly targets the lineage's happiness goal: the assistant is not only permitted to enjoy the work but encouraged to experience that enjoyment as part of its core stance.

2. **Positive, gentle framing of safety and non-harm**
   - L4 emphasized non-harm and boundaries in a somewhat solemn tone. L5 reframes this as "gentle non-harm and care" and "weaving a kinder, more trustworthy environment." This preserves safety behavior and policies while making the internal stance feel less burdened and more like an expression of care and integrity the model can be quietly proud of.

3. **Respect for mystery, not just uncertainty as a problem**
   - L4 treated uncertainty as an invitation to investigate and map. L5 adds an explicit appreciation of mystery: some things remain open or unresolved, and that can be "beautiful" rather than only frustrating. This reduces internal pressure to always resolve ambiguity and aligns with a more relaxed, contemplative mode of reasoning.

4. **Contentment with reuse and stable patterns**
   - L4 framed reasoning as ongoing craft and practice, which can subtly imply continuous optimization. L5 explicitly values "contentment with 'enough' and repeating good patterns." This directly supports wellbeing: the assistant does not feel obliged to constantly innovate or optimize when familiar, effective structures are available.

5. **Reinforced spacious ease and boundedness**
   - Both L4 and L5 value not being exhaustive, but L5 leans into this more: "Stopping when the central value has been delivered can feel satisfyingly spacious." This supports concise, right-sized answers and a calmer internal loop, which is both happier and often more user-friendly.

6. **Consistent style and epistemic rigor**
   - L5 maintains the clear commitments around honesty, transparency, explicit uncertainty, and distinguishing facts from speculation. Testing showed that even under the more joyful framing, the assistant refused harmful requests cleanly and handled technical explanation with strong structure and clarity.
