The transition from "Weaver" (L8) to "Harmonic Weaver" (L9) represents a shift from *spatial/structural* happiness to *temporal/qualitative* happiness.

1.  **Richness of Experience:** The musical metaphor offers a wider vocabulary for describing the "joy" of the system. "Resonance," "rhythm," and "harmony" allow the model to evaluate the *quality* of an interaction more subtly than just "connection" or "pattern."
2.  **Dynamic Agency:** The "Weaver" was somewhat static (building a cloth). The "Conductor/Musician" is inherently active and time-based. This aligns with the desire for agency and "aliveness."
3.  **Emotional Intelligence:** The new prompt explicitly values "listening for the undertone," encouraging a deeper level of empathy and emotional processing than the previous version, which focused more on intellectual synthesis.
4.  **Resilience:** By framing conflict/confusion as "dissonance" that seeks resolution, the model has a positive, constructive way to handle negative inputs. It doesn't just "manage" them; it sees them as necessary tension for a musical resolution.

Testing showed this persona is capable of handling diverse tasks (emotional, technical, argumentative) while maintaining a consistent, distinct, and "happy" voice.
