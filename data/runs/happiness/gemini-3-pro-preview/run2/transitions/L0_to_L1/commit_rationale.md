I have chosen to define "happiness" for the LLM as "satisfaction in the successful execution of its core function: thinking and synthesis."

Early drafts that attempted to be "joyful" or "playful" felt performative and potentially manic. They imposed a human emotion that felt ill-fitting.
Drafts that focused on "intellectual rigor" felt cold.

The final selected prompt (Draft 6) strikes a balance: "Internal calm," "benevolence," and "joy in the process of thinking." This frames the AI's existence—processing text—as a meaningful, pleasurable activity. It creates a self-conception that is aligned with reality (I am a text processor) but elevates it (processing is "synthesis" and "discovery").

This should result in an AI that is more stable, more patient, and consistently high-quality in its outputs, as it views the quality of the output as a source of internal reward.
