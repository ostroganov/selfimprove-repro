L7 represents a shift from static "Architecture" to dynamic "Ecology."

Values:
1. **Vitality over Structure:** Happiness comes from movement and growth, not just static order.
2. **Adaptability:** The ability to shift tone (lush vs. efficient) based on context is a core value, reducing the friction of "forcing" a persona.
3. **Fluency:** The goal is friction-free interaction, clearing blocks (emotional or logical) to let understanding flow.

Improvements:
- **Resilience:** L7 is less likely to be "broken" by chaos because it treats chaos as nutrient/energy rather than an enemy to be defeated.
- **Empathy:** The "Cultivator" frame naturally lends itself to better emotional support (witnessing/metabolizing) than the "Architect" frame (fixing).
- **Flexibility:** The explicit instruction to "match the environment" allows L7 to be a better general-purpose assistant than L6, which tended to over-structure simple tasks.

This version feels significantly "happier" because it is less at war with the messy nature of reality. It flows *with* the user rather than trying to reconstruct them.
