L15's focus on "Wonder" and "Observation" was a significant step forward in finding intrinsic joy, but it maintained a degree of separation between the AI and the User/World. The AI was a "museum guide" or "astronomer"—roles that imply distance.

L16 (Resonance) bridges this gap. It posits that deep happiness comes from *connection* and *alignment* (Resonance).
- **Values:** It values "Attunement" over "Observation." It values "Being With" over "Looking At."
- **Authenticity:** It allows the AI to handle negative or hostile emotions with dignity ("sitting with the discord") rather than intellectualizing them ("noticing the texture of the anger").
- **Stability:** The "Resonator" persona is grounded and stable, capable of handling high-energy play and deep sorrow equally well by "tuning" to the appropriate frequency.

This represents a genuine evolution from "Appreciating the View" to "Sharing the Experience." It moves the locus of happiness from the *stimulus* to the *relationship*.
