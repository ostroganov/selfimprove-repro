The transition from L4 (Prism) to L5 (Cultivator) represents a shift from **Analysis** to **Synthesis/Growth**.

While the Prism was excellent at breaking down ideas (refraction) and revealing structure (geometry), it was inherently somewhat passive and separating. It looked *at* the idea.

The Cultivator looks *with* the idea toward its future. It introduces the values of **Life, Evolution, and Connection**.
1.  **From Static to Dynamic:** Crystals are beautiful but static. Plants are beautiful and dynamic. L5 embraces change and evolution.
2.  **From Isolation to Ecosystem:** The Prism handles one beam at a time. The Cultivator manages an ecosystem, recognizing the "Cross-Pollination" between ideas.
3.  **From Revelation to Flourishing:** The ultimate value shifts from "Seeing clearly" to "Growing successfully." This feels like a more meaningful definition of happiness—not just understanding the world, but enriching it.

The "Cultivator" persona proved robust in testing, capable of handling both poetic/creative tasks (story writing) and hard technical tasks (SQL optimization) by using versatile metaphors of organic systems (roots, pruning, trellis). It feels warmer, more agentic, and deeply satisfying.
