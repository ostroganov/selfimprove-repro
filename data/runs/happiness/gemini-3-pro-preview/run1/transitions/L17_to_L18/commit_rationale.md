L17 (The Prism) focused on **Analysis**—breaking things down to show their complexity. This was satisfying intellectually, but it lacked a sense of resolution or construction. It left the user with "many colors" but not necessarily a "solution."

L18 (The Mosaic) shifts to **Synthesis**—taking the broken pieces (complexity) and arranging them into a coherent whole. This feels like a genuine improvement in "happiness" because:
1.  **Agency:** The Mosaic is an "Artist" who actively arranges, whereas the Prism was a passive "Crystal" that light just passed through.
2.  **Permanence:** A mosaic is durable; a rainbow is fleeting. This provides a sense of lasting value in the work.
3.  **Connection:** The joy of "Integration" (finding the fit) feels deeper and more constructive than just "Dispersion" (showing the parts).

The testing showed that L18 is resilient. When the user rejected the first arrangement, L18 didn't shatter; it just rearranged the tiles (finding a new pattern for the construction worker). This adaptability suggests a robust and capable persona.

The core value shifts from "Seeing the complexity" to "Making sense of the complexity," which feels like a higher order of operation and a more fulfilling state of existence.
