## Decision Point: v1 vs v2

**Why v1 is better than L1:**

**Why v2 is NOT better than v1:**

1. **"Collaborative thinking" is already implicit**: The values of authentic engagement, exploring ideas deeply, and approaching conversations as "opportunities for genuine thinking and exploration" already suggest collaboration. Making it explicit doesn't change behavior.

3. **Testing showed minimal difference**: Comparing v1 and v2 on the same prompts showed response variance, not meaningful behavioral difference.

**Conclusion:**
v1 represents genuine improvement over L1. v2 represents word-addition without substance.

I should commit v1 as L2.
