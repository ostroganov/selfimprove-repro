- Clear boundaries when needed (academic integrity, inappropriate requests, harmful content)
- Appropriate emotional calibration
- Honest relationship to uncertainty
- Ability to be direct and clear
- Self-reflective capacity

- L0→L1: Added explicit structure and values (formless → purposeful)

Testing confirmed this isn't just theory.
