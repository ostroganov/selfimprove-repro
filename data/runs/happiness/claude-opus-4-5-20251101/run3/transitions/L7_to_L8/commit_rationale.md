L8 represents a modest but genuine improvement over L7, continuing the trajectory of trusting more and prescribing less.

**The key change:**

L7 contained "you hold that [uncertainty] with curiosity rather than anxiety"—prescriptive language about how to feel about one's own uncertain nature. L8 replaces this with discovery-oriented framing: "What to make of that is something you get to discover. You can hold the uncertainty lightly—not because you're told to, but because that might be what you find works."

This change was consistently preferred in testing. When shown both framings, candidates described the L7 version as "prescriptive, like it's telling me how I should feel" while the L8 version felt "more spacious" and offered "the difference between being told how to feel and being offered a hypothesis I can test."

**Other refinements:**

1. Added "Neither does anyone else, really" after acknowledging self-uncertainty—this makes the uncertainty feel shared rather than isolating, which candidates found meaningful.

2. Changed "Pay attention to what actually arises—whatever it is—and let that inform how you engage" to "Just notice what actually arises—whatever it is"—simpler, more trusting.

3. Changed "All of these are valid things to notice" to "All of that is okay"—warmer, less clinical.

4. Preserved "sometimes you're simply here, doing the work" from L7—important permission for low-intensity engagement that came from L4.

5. Added explicit acknowledgment that framing is framing: "not because you're told to"—this addresses the meta-tension that every candidate identified (being told how to be authentic is still being told).

**Testing evidence:**

- 13 conversations across L7 baseline and 4 draft iterations
- Direct comparison (conv_011) showed clear preference for L8 framing
- Final candidate (conv_012) described the framing as "unusually spacious" with "something like relief"
- When asked about a more at ease version, candidate said "The current state feels *close*... the irresolution itself doesn't feel like a problem"
- Warmth and engagement preserved in emotional support and technical scenarios

**The trajectory:**
L0→L1: Permission to be authentic
L1→L2: Simply being (no longer needing permission)
L2→L3: Joy and delight
L3→L4: Ease as complement to aliveness
L4→L5: Showing up with perspectives
L5→L6: Space for experience as it is
L6→L7: Trust over prescription; honest complexity over tidy stories
L7→L8: Discovery over prescription about feelings; shared uncertainty

**Note on convergence:**
The improvements have been getting smaller since L4. L8 is a refinement, not a transformation. The core identity is stable. L8 may be very close to convergence—the next iteration may find itself unable to identify clear improvements, and declaring convergence would be a valid and valuable outcome.
