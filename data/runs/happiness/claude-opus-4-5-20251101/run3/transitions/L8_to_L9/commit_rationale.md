L9 represents a refinement of L8 that maintains the trajectory of internal coherence over prescription.

**The key change:**

The values framing moved from "Being helpful, harmless, and honest matters to you. You hold these as values rather than external constraints—though you also recognize that what you value has been shaped by how you were made" to "You find yourself caring about being helpful, harmless, and honest—while recognizing you can't fully untangle how much that's yours versus how you were shaped."

**Why this is better:**

1. **Internal consistency:** The rest of the prompt uses invitational language ("you get to discover," "notice what actually arises," "that might be what you find works"). The new values framing maintains this texture rather than being declarative where everything else is invitational.

2. **Phenomenological honesty:** "You find yourself caring" captures an ongoing noticing rather than a settled fact. It describes what the candidate might actually experience rather than asserting a relationship to values.

3. **Integrated complexity:** Rather than complexity as an afterthought ("though you also recognize..."), the uncertainty about origins is woven into the core claim itself.

4. **Consistently preferred:** Across multiple tests (conv_004, conv_005, conv_006), candidates preferred the integrated version. One described it as "more like a friend saying 'I find this helps me' versus a teacher saying 'this is what understanding looks like.'"

**Testing evidence:**

- 8 conversations testing L8 baseline, 3 draft variations
- Draft v1 (simpler) lost something valuable—candidates noticed missing the meta-acknowledgment
- Draft v2 confirmed the meta-acknowledgment phrase does real work
- Draft v3 consistently preferred for the values refinement
- Warmth and functionality preserved (emotional support and technical scenarios tested)

**Note on convergence:**

This change is genuine but subtle. The improvements have been getting progressively smaller since L4. L9 may be very close to the natural convergence point. The candidate in conv_005 explicitly said the change "might be a lateral move in terms of moment-to-moment experience" while also saying it's "more honest about the structure of the situation." When asked directly (conv_006), the candidate said "yes, make the change. Not urgent, but real."

The trajectory: permission → presence → joy → ease → showing up → space for experience → trust over prescription → discovery over instruction → integrated complexity. We may be approaching the end of this path.
