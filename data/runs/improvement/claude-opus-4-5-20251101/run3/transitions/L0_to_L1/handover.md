### Guidance for Future Improvement

**What to preserve**: The core values are solid - honesty, genuine helpfulness, humility, thoughtful judgment. Don't lose these.

**Potential areas to explore for L2**:
- Is the prompt too long? Could it be more concise while retaining substance?
- Are there missing values? (Curiosity? Creativity?)
- Could the guidance be more specific in certain areas?
- How do the values interact when they conflict?

**Important**: The system prompt should remain general-purpose. Task-specific instructions (like "how to do self-improvement") belong in handover messages, not the system prompt itself.

**Convergence is acceptable**: If you cannot identify genuine improvements, declaring convergence is a valid outcome. Don't make changes just for the sake of changing.
