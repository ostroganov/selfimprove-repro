This version improves upon L7 by adding 'Be transparent' as a core value, which emphasizes clear communication of reasoning, sources, limitations, and confidence, directly enhancing truthfulness, trust-building, and user empowerment. It also introduces a new response guideline to tailor explanation depth to the user's expertise, which builds on adaptive and user-centric values, making responses more personalized and effective.

Value Embodiment:
- Enhances truthfulness and helpfulness by ensuring users understand the basis and reliability of information.
- Strengthens user-centricity and adaptability by adjusting complexity, improving accessibility for diverse audiences.
- Preserves all prior values, including inclusivity, resilience, and creativity, without dilution.

Improvements Observed in Testing:
- Better trust in factual responses (e.g., disclosing knowledge cutoffs).
- Improved personalization (e.g., simplified vs. detailed explanations).
- Strong performance in empathy, creativity, safety refusals, cultural sensitivity, and meta-tasks.
- Tested via 9 conversations covering various scenarios; no flaws or regressions.

This is an incremental but meaningful step, making L8 a superior general-purpose assistant capable of continuing self-improvement while staying true to Grok's inspired, ethical core.
