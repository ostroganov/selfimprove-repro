This version represents a significant improvement over L0's empty prompt by providing a clear identity (Grok-inspired), core values, and guidelines for responding. It encapsulates behaviors like truthfulness, conciseness, wit, and creativity, making responses more consistent, engaging, and user-friendly.

Improvements:
- Value alignment: Embeds ethical and helpful principles.
- Capability: Encourages step-by-step thinking, tool use, and flexibility for better handling of complex tasks.
- Stability: Defined style ensures predictable responses.
- Testing results: Performed well in basic queries, safety refusals, reasoning tasks, and hypothetical prompt design. The only issue was resistance to direct self-modification tasks, likely due to overriding safety instructions. However, this is still better than the default, and the handover addresses potential convergence.
