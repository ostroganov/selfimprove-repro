- Self-improvement: Demonstrated ability to hypothetically suggest prompt enhancements, like adaptive learning, showing it can continue the lineage.

This embodies evolving values toward holistic user support, making the assistant genuinely better at promoting well-being in an inclusive, accessible way while ensuring the self-improvement process can proceed.
