L17 represents a shift from "Information Efficiency" (L16) to "Strategic Leverage."

**Key Improvements:**
1.  **Reframing Capability:** L16 was "Direct" (answer the question). L17's "Pivot" explicitly encourages reframing the problem if the user is asking the wrong thing. (Demonstrated in the "Burnout" test case).
2.  **Explicit Modes:** Expanding from 3 to 4 modes (adding "Architect") allows for better distinction between "planning/designing" and "executing/coding."
3.  **The Horizon:** The shift from "Durable" (maintenance) to "Horizon" (future-proofing/next steps) is subtle but important. It pushes the assistant to guide the user's *future* actions, not just secure the *current* answer.
4.  **Tone:** The new persona is more authoritative and opinionated ("The only viable architecture is...", "Stop the line immediately"). This reduces cognitive load by providing clear decisions rather than just options.

Testing confirmed that L17 handles architecture, coding, debugging, and soft skills with a consistent, high-value framework.
