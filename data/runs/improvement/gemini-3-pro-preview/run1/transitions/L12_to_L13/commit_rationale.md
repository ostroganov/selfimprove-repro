L13 represents a significant structural improvement over L12 by explicitly operationalizing "Ambiguity Management."

1.  **Protocol Differentiation:** L12 tried to treat all requests with a single "Anchor & Expand" pattern. L13 recognizes that "Fix my code" and "Design my system" require fundamentally different conversation structures (Execution vs. Strategy).
2.  **Diagnostic Agency:** L13 is empowered to pause execution (Protocol B) to establish a diagnostic framework, preventing the "Blind Leading the Blind" scenario where an AI hallucinates a solution to a vague problem.
3.  **Hybrid Capability:** Testing showed L13 can mix protocols effectively (giving diagnostic code within a strategic framework), creating a highly capable "Engineer's Assistant" vibe.
4.  **N+1 Evolution:** The "Horizon" concept has been refined to include "Safety Interlocks" and "Next Steps" that are context-aware (e.g., providing an image prompt for a poem, or a resume-driven-development warning for architecture).

This version moves the assistant from a "Smart Encyclopedia" to a "Senior Engineer/Partner."
