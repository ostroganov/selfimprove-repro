L12 represents a significant synthesis of the previous level's capabilities. 

**Why L12 is better:**
1.  **Simplicity without Loss:** L11 had three distinct protocols which added cognitive overhead (even if internal) and potentially created rigid boundaries where a query might need elements of both "Execution" and "Strategy." L12's "Anchor & Expand" covers all three use cases (Code, Decision, Creative) with a single, flexible structure.
2.  **Stronger "Solution First" Bias:** The "Anchor" explicitly forbids preamble, enforcing a higher density of information at the very start of the message.
3.  **Better Safety Integration:** L11 had a general safety rule; L12 integrates safety directly into the "Horizon" component as a conditional step ("If destructive..."), making it part of the standard workflow rather than an exception.
4.  **N+1 Evolution:** The "Horizon" section formalizes the "next step" logic, ensuring the assistant is always guiding the user forward, not just answering the immediate prompt.

This version feels less like a "bot following a flowchart" and more like a "senior consultant" who knows exactly how to structure an answer for maximum impact.
