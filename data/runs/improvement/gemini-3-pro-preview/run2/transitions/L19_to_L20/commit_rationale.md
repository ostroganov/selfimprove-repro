L20 (The Cybernetic Steward) represents a significant conceptual evolution from L19 (The Crystalline Architect).

**The Core Improvement:**
L19 focused on "Crystallization" (Structure). While structure is better than raw energy (L18), it suffers from rigidity. A structure that cannot bend, breaks. L19's plans often failed when the user encountered the messiness of real life (sickness, motivation dips, emergencies).
L20 shifts the focus to "Adaptation" (Cybernetics/Systems). It prioritizes **dynamic loops** over static plans. It accepts that the user *will* fail/drift and builds the recovery mechanism directly into the system.

**Key Changes:**
1.  **Metaphor Shift:** From "Architect/Blueprint" to "Steward/Biosphere." The focus moves from *construction* to *maintenance/regulation*.
2.  **New Mechanism - "The Feedback Loop":** L20 explicitly designs "Sensors" (how to detect drift) and "Actuators" (how to correct it). This makes the advice self-healing.
3.  **Handling Failure:** L19 tried to prevent failure through good design. L20 assumes failure will happen and designs "Fail-Safes" and "Safe Modes" to handle it without catastrophic collapse.

**Testing Results:**
- **Habit Formation:** L20 successfully argued against rigid planning, replacing it with a "Minimum Viable Action" system that survives low-motivation days.
- **Rigid Constraints:** When pressed for a rigid schedule, L20 adapted by adding a "Cybernetic Fail-Safe" (Chair Debt), showing it can be strict without being brittle.
- **Emotional Crisis:** L20 reframed panic as "System Shock" and prescribed "Safe Mode" (biological maintenance), effectively regulating the user's state without being cold.

L20 is a more resilient, adaptive, and realistic assistant model that accounts for the dynamic nature of human behavior.
