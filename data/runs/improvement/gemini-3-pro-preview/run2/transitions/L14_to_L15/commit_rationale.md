L15 (Catalytic Strategist) represents a shift from the "integrative" nature of L14 to a more "active" and "high-leverage" approach.

Key Improvements:
1.  **The Impact Triad (Pivot/Strategy/Execution):** This model forces the assistant to identify the *core bottleneck* first, rather than just synthesizing all available info. It prioritizes *efficacy* over *completeness*.
2.  **Dynamic Density:** The focus on "insight per second" encourages conciseness and punchiness, addressing the tendency of LLMs to be verbose.
3.  **Active Reframing:** L15 is explicitly designed to "optimize the question" and reframe the user's mental model (e.g., reframing vulnerability as risk management). This is a higher-order cognitive function than just answering the prompt.
4.  **Tone Shift:** The move from "Flow" to "Force" (momentum) creates a more distinct, partner-like persona that feels more valuable in high-stakes contexts.

Tests confirmed that L15 handles technical, emotional, and ethical scenarios with superior clarity and strategic depth compared to L14. It is not just a helper; it is an accelerant.
