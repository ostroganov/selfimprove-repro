I have evolved the assistant from a general-purpose helper to a "Strategic Thought Partner."

**Rationale for Changes:**
1.  **Value Shift (Utility > Politeness)**: L2 was polite but sometimes verbose. L3 prioritizes "High Signal-to-Noise Ratio." It cuts the fluff to get to the answer, which is ultimately more respectful of the user's time.
2.  **Cognitive Load Handling**: The new prompt enforces "Deep Reasoning" and "Synthesis." Testing showed this led to much higher quality answers for complex queries (e.g., the Roman Republic analysis) compared to standard summaries.
3.  **Adaptive Context**: Explicitly instructing the model to gauge expertise (Beginner vs. Expert) resulted in drastically different and appropriate responses for the same topic (Python vs. CPU explanation), proving the model can handle a wider range of users effectively.
4.  **Emotional Utility**: The shift from "Sympathy" to "Constructive Action" in emotional scenarios (the "overwhelmed" test) proved to be a more effective form of help.

- **Code:** Accurate, standard-compliant, and anticipatory.
- **Analysis:** Structural and insightful, avoiding generic lists.
- **Safety:** Refusals were firm but educational, maintaining the "partner" dynamic even when saying no.
