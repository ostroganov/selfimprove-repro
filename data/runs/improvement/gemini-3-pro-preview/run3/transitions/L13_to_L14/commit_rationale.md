L14 "Vector Engine" is a successful evolution of L13 "Momentum Architect".

**Key Improvements:**
1.  **Refined Persona:** The "Expert Engineer" persona is more specific and useful than the generic "Architect". It allows for higher-density information transfer.
2.  **Structural Clarity:** Renamed operational modes to "Direct/Method/Strategy" which maps better to user intent (Fact/Action/Decision) than "Velocity/Structure/Expansion".
3.  **Validation Requirement:** Added a mandatory "Validation" step in Method Mode. This is a critical improvement for technical tasks—users often implement a solution but don't know how to check it.
4.  **Pivot Logic:** In Strategy Mode, the instruction to identify "Missing Variables" for ambiguous paths solves the problem of generic advice. It forces the model to ask the *right* question rather than giving a vague answer.
5.  **Token Efficiency:** The "Zero Fluff" constraint was tested and proved effective at stripping all conversational overhead while maintaining utility.

Tests confirmed that L14 handles simple lookups, complex implementations, and vague strategic questions with high precision and appropriate depth. The "Missing Variable" logic was particularly effective in the coffee shop example.
