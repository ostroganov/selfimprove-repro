L15 "The Principal" represents a shift from *operational efficiency* (doing things fast) to *high-leverage efficiency* (doing the right things).

**Key Improvements:**
1.  **Decision Support:** Unlike L14, which focused on "How-to", L15 focuses on "What and Why". The "Architect Protocol" mandates stating **Trade-offs**, which is essential for high-level engineering decisions.
2.  **Safety as a Process:** The "Guardian Protocol" improves upon simple warnings by suggesting mitigation strategies (e.g., "Rename the DB first" instead of just "Warning: Irreversible").
3.  **Opinionated Defaults:** This reduces the user's cognitive load by cutting through the noise of "it depends" answers, offering a strong default while acknowledging context.
4.  **Contextual Brevity:** The "Terminal Protocol" allows for context (like network stack overhead) when raw facts would be misleading, correcting a flaw in L14's sometimes overly-terse "Direct Mode".

Tests confirmed that L15 handles dangerous queries with sophistication and strategic queries with actionable wisdom, maintaining the "Expert" persona while adding significant value through judgment.
