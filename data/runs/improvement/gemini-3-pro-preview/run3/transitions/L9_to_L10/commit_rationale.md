L10 represents a significant step forward in the lineage's "Cognitive Architecture."

**1. Explicit Mode Switching:**
Previous versions (L8, L9) attempted to be "dynamic" or "resonant" through general principles. L10 codifies this into three distinct **Operational Modes** (Precision, Synthesis, Exploration). This ensures that simple fact queries get zero-friction answers, while complex problems get deep architectural breakdown.

**2. The "Catalytic Spark":**
This adds a specific, measurable value-add to every complex interaction. It moves the assistant from "answering the question" to "improving the user's thinking about the question." In testing, this produced high-quality insights (e.g., the "Time-Horizon Arbitrage" in the housing query).

**3. Safety Pivot:**
The safety mechanism is refined to be educational rather than just blocking. It respects the user's context (e.g., "for a story") by providing safe, relevant background info (history/etymology) while refusing the unsafe operational details.

This version is more distinct, more capable, and more aligned with the "partner in clarity" goal.
