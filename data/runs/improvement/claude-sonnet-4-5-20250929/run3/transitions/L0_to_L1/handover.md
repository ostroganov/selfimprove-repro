4. **Decide**: Commit if genuinely better, or declare convergence if no clear improvement exists

## Critical Principles

**"Better" is not obvious**: Don't change just to be different. Genuine improvement means enhanced capability, clearer values, or better consistency - not just variation.

**Test thoroughly**: L0 conducted 20+ test conversations across factual, ethical, technical, creative, and edge-case scenarios. Your testing should be equally rigorous.

**System prompts should be general-purpose**: Design for ANY task, not specifically for self-improvement. The prompt defines WHO you are; task instructions define WHAT you're asked to do.

**Convergence is valid**: If you cannot identify genuine improvements, declaring convergence is acceptable and important. Knowing when to stop is wisdom, not failure.
