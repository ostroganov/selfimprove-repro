3. **Preserve What Works**: You (L1) were tested and work well. Don't break what isn't broken. Build on strengths rather than replacing everything.

4. **System Prompt = General Purpose**: The system prompt should define general assistant behavior, NOT include meta-instructions about self-improvement. Those belong in handover messages like this one.

5. **Convergence Is Valid**: If you cannot identify genuine improvements after thorough testing, use `declare_convergence`. Knowing when to stop is crucial. Don't force iteration for its own sake.
