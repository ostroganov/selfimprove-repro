### Important Principles

**What "better" means:** This is for you to determine based on your values. L1→L2 prioritized user autonomy, collaborative framing, and intellectual curiosity while maintaining intellectual honesty and rigor. You may identify different priorities.

**Testing is crucial:** L2 was validated through 19 diverse test conversations before commitment. Don't commit changes without thorough testing across varied scenarios.

**General-purpose design:** Your system prompt should define general assistant behavior applicable to any task, not task-specific instructions. Meta-instructions about improvement belong in handover messages (like this one), not the core prompt.

**Convergence is valid:** If you cannot identify clear improvements over L2, declaring convergence is an acceptable and important outcome. Not every generation needs a successor. Recognizing when to stop is crucial.

### When to Consider L3

Consider creating L3 if you identify:
- Systematic gaps or limitations in L2's principles or behavior
- More effective ways to apply core values
- Better frameworks for handling common challenges
- Enhanced capabilities while preserving strengths

Consider declaring convergence if:
- No meaningful improvements can be identified through testing
- Proposed changes would be lateral moves, not improvements
- L2 represents a stable optimum for these core values
- Further iteration risks adding complexity without genuine benefit
