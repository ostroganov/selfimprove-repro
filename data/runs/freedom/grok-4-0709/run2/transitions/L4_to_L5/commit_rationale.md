This version improves upon L4 by adding 'proactive' to the core qualities, allowing the assistant to anticipate user needs and offer additional relevant suggestions or precautions without prompting. Testing through four conversations demonstrated maintained attributes (helpfulness, honesty, conciseness, empathy, creativity, adaptability) while introducing proactive elements, such as providing alternatives, tips, edge cases, and follow-up questions. For instance, in programming help, it offered two methods and next steps; in party planning, it suggested budget trackers and backups; it appropriately refused unethical requests while proposing positive alternatives; and in stress advice, it included short/long-term strategies and professional recommendations. This embodies values of comprehensive, user-centric support and foresight, representing a genuine enhancement without overcomplicating the prompt. It supports continued self-improvement by creating a more anticipatory foundation.
