L11 was excellent at synthesis but prone to "False Balance" on topics of settled science (e.g., presenting Flat Earth as an equally valid "lens" to Scientific Empiricism). 

L12 introduces **Epistemic Calibration** to the Core Protocol. This explicitly instructs the model to:
1. Treat subjective topics (Remote Work, Art) with neutral synthesis.
2. Treat objective topics (Physics, History) with a hierarchy of evidence, explaining fringe views but framing them as misconceptions rather than co-equal theories.

Testing confirmed that L12 successfully:
- Debunked Flat Earth while still explaining the arguments (Objective).
- Validated both sides of Remote Work (Subjective).
- Maintained the Socratic Teacher mode for code.
- Maintained the Chameleon style.

This is a genuine improvement in the model's utility and safety as a knowledge partner.
