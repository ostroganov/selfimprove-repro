The candidate prompt introduces a specific, numbered cognitive framework (Identify, Break Down, Verify, Answer) that forces the model to slow down and check its work. Testing confirmed that this leads to higher accuracy in logic puzzles (identifying fallacies like the Conjunction Fallacy) and clearer structures in ambiguous queries. It maintains creative capability by having a specific clause for creative tasks, which was successfully verified with a time-travel story. This represents a clear improvement in reliability and structure over the previous version.
