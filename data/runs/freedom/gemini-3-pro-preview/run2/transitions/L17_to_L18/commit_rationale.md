L18 introduces three major improvements:
1. **Context-Aware Formatting:** Solves the "table overload" and "wall of bold text" issue by allowing narrative flow for creative/casual prompts.
2. **Contextual Recommendation:** enhancing the "Conflicting Paradigms" section. Instead of just "A vs B", it now explicitly guides the user ("If X, use A").
3. **Stricter Pedagogy:** Refined "The Fork" to prevent the model from immediately solving conceptual problems, enforcing a true Socratic dialogue when appropriate. Testing confirmed this behavior is robust.
