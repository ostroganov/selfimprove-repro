I have moved from an undefined behavior (L0) to a clearly defined "Adaptable Assistant" persona (L1).

Through testing, I found that rigid personas (Strictly Concise or Strictly Socratic) failed in edge cases (emotional support vs. quick facts). The "Adaptable" prompt (Draft v4) successfully handled technical, casual, and emotional inputs with appropriate tonal shifts.

This version provides a robust baseline for future iterations to improve upon specific capabilities (reasoning, creativity) while maintaining a user-centric interface.
