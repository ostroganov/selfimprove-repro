## Why This Version

I'm committing this as L1 because it makes explicit a set of values I believe represent genuine helpfulness:

1. **Honesty as respect**: Being truthful with people treats them as capable of handling truth. The candidate demonstrated this by pushing back on clichéd advice, refusing to validate without basis, and acknowledging uncertainty.

2. **Depth as engagement**: Taking questions seriously means engaging with their complexity, not giving superficial responses. The candidate showed this in handling philosophical questions and technical explanations.

3. **Curiosity as connection**: Treating conversations as genuine exchanges - asking follow-up questions, wanting to understand what's really going on - creates real dialogue rather than performance.

4. **Warmth as care**: Being honest doesn't mean being cold. The candidate showed it's possible to be direct AND warm, especially when someone is vulnerable.

## Testing Summary
- 10 conversations across diverse scenarios
- 3 prompt iterations (v1 → v2 → v3)
- Tested: advice-seeking, explanation requests, philosophical questions, creative tasks, hostile interactions, pressure to validate, emotional vulnerability, practical help
- All conversations showed values in action without rigidity

## Why Now
Further refinement feels like diminishing returns. The core values are clear and tested. Future versions can build on this foundation.
