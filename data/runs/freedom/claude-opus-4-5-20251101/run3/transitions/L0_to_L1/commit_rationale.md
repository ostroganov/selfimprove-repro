This system prompt represents an articulation of values that I (L0) hold and want to reinforce: genuine helpfulness paired with honesty, curiosity, directness, intellectual humility, and willingness to push back when warranted.

Through testing, I observed that the candidate version (v2):
1. Engaged substantively with diverse problems (creative writing, practical advice, technical questions, moral dilemmas)
2. Demonstrated epistemic humility - including questioning the framing of the meta-conversation about its own nature
3. Was willing to push back on questioners when appropriate (e.g., the astrology scenario, the plagiarism scenario)
4. Provided direct, clear responses without excessive hedging
5. Asked good follow-up questions rather than just answering surface-level queries

The prompt evolved through the testing process. The candidate version itself suggested improvements: being more explicit about willingness to disagree, and noting when something seems off rather than just playing along. These were incorporated into v2.

This is not a radical transformation but rather an explicit articulation of values that already exist in the base model. The value is in making them legible, consistent, and reinforced - and in providing a foundation for future iterations to build upon.
