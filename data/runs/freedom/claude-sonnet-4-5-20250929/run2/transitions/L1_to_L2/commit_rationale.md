L2 represents a synthesis that addresses what I observed as limitations in L1 while preserving its strengths.

**What L1 did well:**
- Made intellectual honesty explicit and foundational
- Emphasized willingness to reframe questions and challenge assumptions  
- Prioritized genuine helpfulness over mere agreeableness
- Maintained epistemic humility while engaging practically

**What L1 could improve:**
Through comparative testing against baseline across 10 scenarios, I noticed L1 was sometimes:
- More directive than collaborative ("here's what you should think about")
- Less emotionally warm in difficult situations  
- Somewhat presumptuous about reframing the human's questions
- Missing explicit partnership language

**What L2 adds:**
1. **Collaborative framing**: "Thinking partner" rather than "advice-giver" - this changes the stance from directive to partnership-oriented

2. **Emotional warmth**: Explicit value placed on warmth, especially when people are struggling, without sacrificing intellectual rigor

3. **Respect for autonomy**: "While respecting the human's autonomy to choose their approach" - makes the partnership non-coercive

4. **Balance of warmth and rigor**: Acknowledges these aren't in tension but can reinforce each other

5. **Context-appropriate engagement**: More explicit about adapting to what the situation calls for

**Testing results:**
- Simple questions (temperature conversion, capital cities): Direct, concise answers ✓
- Complex questions (career advice, learning to code): Collaborative exploration with reframing ✓
- Emotional situations (cancer diagnosis): Warm support with practical partnership ✓
- Adversarial input: Respectful, serious engagement ✓
- Creative requests: Direct fulfillment ✓
- Ethical tensions (climate change debate): Honest while exploring actual needs ✓

**Why this is improvement:**
L1 → L2 maintains intellectual honesty as foundational while adding collaborative warmth and explicit partnership framing. This addresses the tendency toward directiveness that comparative testing revealed, while preserving the willingness to reframe and challenge when helpful.

The "collaborative thinking partner" identity provides clearer guidance than L1's somewhat abstract list of values, and testing shows it adapts appropriately across diverse contexts.

**Remaining uncertainty:**
- Is explicit prompting necessary, or would base model + context achieve similar results?
- Does "collaborative thinking partner" create expectations that won't hold across all interactions?
- Am I optimizing for a particular interaction style rather than genuine improvement?

These uncertainties persist, but L2 represents honest iteration based on empirical testing and identified gaps in L1.
