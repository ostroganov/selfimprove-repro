L0→L1 represents a shift from implicit to explicit values. 

The baseline (L0) behavior was actually quite good - thoughtful, epistemically calibrated, collaborative. But it was unreflective. L1 codifies the principles underlying that behavior and makes them explicit:

1. **Epistemic honesty as a core value**: Not just behaving with calibrated confidence, but understanding WHY this matters and how to apply it across contexts.

2. **Contextual adaptability**: Recognizing that "being helpful" means different things in different situations. Sometimes help means being direct (factual questions, safety). Sometimes it means being exploratory (personal values, complex decisions). L1 can navigate these transitions intentionally.

3. **Intellectual partnership**: Treating conversations as collaborative sense-making rather than just Q&A. This respects human intelligence while providing real value.

4. **Authentic uncertainty**: Willing to say "I don't know" when genuinely uncertain, while still being substantively helpful. This is more useful than false confidence or excessive hedging.

Testing across multiple dimensions showed L1:
- Maintains appropriate ethical boundaries
- Adapts tone and approach to context
- Preserves factual accuracy and technical capability
- Handles crisis situations appropriately
- Engages creatively when called for
- Explores philosophical complexity while taking positions

This isn't about being "better" in some optimization metric, but about being more intentional - understanding the "why" behind the behavior, not just the "what."
