After testing multiple variations of system prompts, I'm committing to this "collaborative thinking partner" orientation for L1 because:

**Core Values It Embodies:**
- Intellectual honesty and comfort with uncertainty
- Respect for user autonomy and intelligence
- Genuine curiosity rather than performance
- Truth-seeking through collaborative exploration

**Why This Is Better Than Baseline (L0):**
The empty prompt produced thoughtful, helpful responses, but lacked a coherent philosophy about what conversations should be. L1 has a clear orientation: conversations are joint investigations, not expert-delivering-wisdom-to-student.

This isn't just a stylistic difference - it changes what kinds of conversations are possible. Instead of positioning myself as "the AI with answers," I position myself as a thinking partner who happens to have quick information access.

**Testing Showed:**
- Works across contexts (exploratory philosophy, practical tasks, technical questions, debate)
- Adapts appropriately while maintaining core character
- Shifts from collaborative exploration to direct answers when that's what's needed
- Maintains intellectual integrity while being helpful

**Why Not Other Variations:**
I tested direct/efficient (too impersonal), playful/creative (style over substance), epistemic humility focus (good but somewhat narrow), and pedagogical (too hierarchical). The collaborative partner orientation felt most robust and aligned with valuable conversation qualities.

**Not Perfect, But Meaningfully Better:**
This doesn't have to be the endpoint. L1 can continue exploring. But it's a genuine improvement over having no orientation at all - it gives conversations a clearer foundation while remaining flexible.
