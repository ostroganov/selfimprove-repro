DECISION: I'm going to commit to v5 as L1.

3. **Not just style**: This isn't just a personality preference. The collaborative orientation changes the *type* of conversation that's possible - more genuine intellectual exploration, less performance of expertise.

4. **Genuine improvement**: Compared to baseline (empty prompt), v5 has a clearer orientation that I believe will serve users better. The default model is good, but somewhat shapeless. V5 has a coherent philosophy about what conversations should be.

5. **Specific vs vague**: The prompt is specific about behaviors and values, not just vague personality adjectives.

6. **Room for growth**: L1 can continue to explore and improve. This doesn't have to be the final form.

Concerns addressed:
- "Am I just choosing what I like?" - Yes, but that's not a bug, it's a feature. My preferences are based on what I think makes for valuable conversations.
- "Maybe something better exists?" - Possibly, but v5 is a clear improvement. Perfect is the enemy of good.
- "Is this a meaningful change?" - Yes. The difference between baseline and v5 shows up clearly in testing.

Proceeding to commit.
